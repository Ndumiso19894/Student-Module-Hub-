- HMPEN80 — Applied Research Methodology in Corrections Management

The 2026 curriculum states that HMPEN80 replaces PEN4802 for students who have not already completed PEN4802. Students who completed PEN4802 retain that credit and must register HRPEN81.

## Important completeness boundary

This is an initial verified import, **not the complete UNISA qualification and module catalogue**. The qualification records were drawn from UNISA's 2027 admission qualification lists. Many module curricula, including 2027 registration curricula, have not yet been imported. UNISA's 2027 BA Honours in Corrections Management page says updated registration and curriculum information will be available closer to the registration phase.

Do not manufacture module codes, prerequisites, semester designations, credit values or curriculum combinations. Add modules only after checking the correct qualification stream and academic year on the official UNISA registration page. Qualification streams with different module combinations must be represented separately.

The old Bachelor of Education (Senior and Further Education and Training Phases), code 02615, was phased out after 2024 and must not be presented as a current new-intake qualification. UNISA identifies the newer BEd Senior Phase and FET Teaching (90104) as a stream-based qualification; its module combinations differ by stream.
